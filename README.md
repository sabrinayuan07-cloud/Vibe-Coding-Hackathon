## Surviving UBC - Campus Study Spot Finder
A collaborative web platform to help UBC students discover, review, and navigate the best study spots across campus.

# Inspiration
Every UBC student has experienced the frustration of wandering around campus looking for the perfect study spot - only to find it crowded, noisy, or closed. We built Surviving UBC to solve this universal student struggle by crowdsourcing knowledge about study spaces across campus, helping students spend less time searching and more time studying.
# What It Does
Surviving UBC is your ultimate guide to finding the perfect study environment on campus:

- Discover Study Spots: Browse study locations across different UBC buildings with detailed information about each space
- Community Reviews: Read authentic reviews from fellow students about noise levels, outlets, seating, WiFi quality, and overall vibe
- Share Your Experience: Contribute by writing reviews to help other students make informed decisions
- Interactive Campus Map: Visualize study spot locations on an interactive map and navigate directly to them
- Smart Filtering: Find exactly what you need - quiet corners for focus sessions or collaborative spaces for group work
